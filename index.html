Our story

Every step is
a victory.

Life is full of changes and challenges. No matter what comes your way, what matters is to keep moving forward. That belief has a name in Burmese — ဆက်လျှောက် — keep walking.

We are a small team in Bangkok knitting socks, tees and pins for a big reason: walking beside comrades and friends in Myanmar who lost limbs to conflict-related injuries. Every pair you wear supports their next step — recovery, prosthetic care and the simple promise that no one walks alone.

Our logo says it best: the word ဆက်လျှောက် itself stands up and walks — on one natural leg and one prosthetic leg, both wearing the same sneaker, heading the same way.

Why we walk

Some of us walk on carbon and steel.
All of us walk together.

These photographs are our community — friends who lost limbs and kept going. When you shop with us you support their network, their recovery, and you help raise awareness of what is happening in Myanmar. We are still here. Still fighting for each other. Still walking the same path, all together.
